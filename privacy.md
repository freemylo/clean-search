# Skimless privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Skimless is a browser extension that hides AI Overviews, AI Mode and other parts of Google Search results pages that you choose to hide.

## What Skimless collects

**Nothing.** Skimless does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, search terms or usage data. It contains no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

## What stays on your device

- **Your settings** (which parts are switched on or off) are saved with Chrome's own storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them to your other computers. Skimless's developer cannot see them.
- **A list of page elements to hide** is saved in your browser.

## The one network request Skimless makes

About every 6 hours, Skimless downloads a small public file (`selectors.json`) from GitHub at
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/freemylo/clean-search/main/selectors/selectors.json`.
It lists which parts of the Google page to hide, so fixes can arrive quickly when Google changes its page. The file contains data only, never code.

The request includes no information about you, your settings or your searches, and no cookies. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the file; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## How Skimless works on Google Search

Skimless runs only on Google Search pages (google.com and google.co.uk). It changes how the page looks inside your browser by hiding parts of it. It does not read, record or send what you search for.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
